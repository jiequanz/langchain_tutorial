HELIOS BIKES — WARRANTY AND RETURNS

WARRANTY COVERAGE
Helios bikes are covered by a tiered warranty:

- Frame: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects for the original owner.
- Battery (the Helios PowerCell): 2-year warranty. A battery is considered
  defective if it drops below 70 percent of its original capacity within the
  warranty period under normal use.
- Motor and electronics: 2-year warranty.
- Other parts (brakes, tires, drivetrain): 1-year warranty against defects.
  Normal wear and tear is not covered.

The warranty does not cover damage from accidents, misuse, unauthorized
modifications, or failure to follow the battery care guidelines.

RETURNS
New bikes may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, as long
as the bike is in original condition with less than 50 miles of use. Return
shipping for online orders is free. Bikes purchased in a retail store can be
returned to any Helios store.

Accessories and replacement batteries may be returned within 30 days if unused
and in original packaging.

To start a warranty claim or a return, contact customer support or visit any
Helios retail store. Service appointments and store hours are listed in our
support information.
