HELIOS BIKES — COMPANY OVERVIEW

Helios Bikes is an electric bicycle company founded in 2018 in Portland,
Oregon. The company was started by two former cycling-team engineers, Mara
Quinn and Daniel Osei, who wanted to make electric bikes that were practical
for everyday commuting rather than just recreation.

Our mission is to replace short car trips with clean, affordable electric
rides. The average Helios customer uses their bike for trips under five miles
that they previously made by car.

Helios is headquartered in Portland and operates retail stores in Portland,
Seattle, and San Francisco. All of our bikes are assembled in the United
States from a mix of domestic and imported components.

As of 2025, Helios has sold more than 80,000 bikes and employs around 140
people across engineering, manufacturing, retail, and customer support. The
company is privately held and is not publicly traded.

We offer three bike models — the Dart, the Trailblazer, and the Hauler — which
are described in our product catalog. All models share the same battery
platform and charging system, which keeps maintenance and replacement parts
simple across the lineup.
