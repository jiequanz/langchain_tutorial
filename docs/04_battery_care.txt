HELIOS BIKES — BATTERY CARE GUIDE

The Helios PowerCell battery is shared across all Helios models. Following these
guidelines keeps it healthy and keeps your warranty valid.

CHARGING
Use only the charger supplied with your bike. A full charge takes about 4 hours.
You do not need to fully drain the battery before recharging; lithium batteries
last longer with partial, frequent charges than with deep discharges. Avoid
leaving the battery on the charger continuously for more than 24 hours.

TEMPERATURE
Charge and store the battery at room temperature when possible. Do not charge
the battery below freezing (32 F) or above 110 F. Charging a cold battery can
cause permanent capacity loss.

WINTER STORAGE
If you will not ride for a month or more, remove the battery and store it
indoors at roughly 50 percent charge. Check it every few weeks and top it back
up to 50 percent if it drifts low. Do not store a fully empty battery, as it can
fall into a deep-discharge state that the charger cannot recover.

LIFESPAN
Under normal use, the PowerCell retains at least 70 percent of its capacity for
about 800 charge cycles, which is roughly three to five years for most riders.
A battery that drops below 70 percent within the 2-year warranty window may be
eligible for a warranty replacement.
