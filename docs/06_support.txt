HELIOS BIKES — STORES AND CUSTOMER SUPPORT

RETAIL STORES
Helios operates three retail stores. All three offer sales, test rides, service,
and white-glove delivery scheduling.

- Portland: 412 SE Hawthorne Blvd. Open Monday-Saturday 10am-7pm, Sunday 11am-5pm.
- Seattle: 88 Pine Street. Open Monday-Saturday 10am-7pm, closed Sunday.
- San Francisco: 1500 Valencia Street. Open daily 10am-6pm.

SERVICE APPOINTMENTS
Tune-ups, repairs, and warranty service are handled at any retail store by
appointment. Same-day service is sometimes available but is not guaranteed; we
recommend booking ahead. The first annual tune-up is free for the first two
years of ownership.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT
Phone and email support are available Monday-Friday, 8am-6pm Pacific Time.
- Phone: 1-800-555-RIDE
- Email: support@heliosbikes.example

Use customer support for order tracking, warranty claims, returns, and general
questions. Riders outside our three store cities can mail in batteries or small
parts for warranty service; support will send a prepaid shipping label.

ONLINE
Order status, manuals, and the battery care guide are available in your online
account. Live chat is available on the website during support hours.
