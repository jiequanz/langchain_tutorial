HELIOS BIKES — PRODUCT CATALOG

Helios offers three electric bike models. All three use the same Helios PowerCell
battery and the same charger, but they differ in frame, range, and purpose.

THE DART (Commuter)
The Dart is our lightweight city commuter, weighing 38 pounds. It has a range
of up to 40 miles per charge and a top assisted speed of 20 mph. It is designed
for flat-to-moderate terrain and daily commuting. Price: $1,800.

THE TRAILBLAZER (Mountain)
The Trailblazer is built for off-road and hilly terrain. It has wider tires,
front suspension, and a more powerful motor. Its range is up to 35 miles per
charge, with a top assisted speed of 20 mph. It weighs 52 pounds. Price: $2,600.

THE HAULER (Cargo)
The Hauler is our cargo bike, designed to carry up to 150 pounds of cargo plus
the rider. It has an extended rear rack and a reinforced frame. Its range is up
to 30 miles per charge because of the heavier load. It weighs 68 pounds.
Price: $3,200.

BATTERY PLATFORM
Every model ships with the same removable Helios PowerCell battery, rated at
500 watt-hours. A full charge takes about 4 hours. Because the battery is shared
across all models, a replacement battery fits any Helios bike. Battery care and
charging guidance are covered in a separate maintenance guide.
