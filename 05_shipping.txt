HELIOS BIKES — SHIPPING AND DELIVERY

WHERE WE SHIP
Helios ships to all 50 U.S. states. We do not currently ship internationally.

DELIVERY TIMES
Standard delivery takes 5 to 8 business days from the date your order ships.
Orders are usually processed within 1 to 2 business days of being placed.

SHIPPING COST
Shipping is free on all bike orders nationwide. Accessories ordered on their own
ship for a flat fee of $8.

ASSEMBLY
Bikes arrive about 90 percent assembled. The customer attaches the front wheel,
handlebars, pedals, and seat, which takes most people 20 to 30 minutes with the
included tools. A printed and video guide is included.

WHITE-GLOVE DELIVERY
In the three cities where we have retail stores — Portland, Seattle, and San
Francisco — you can choose free white-glove delivery instead. With white-glove
delivery, a Helios technician brings the bike fully assembled, adjusts it to fit
you, and walks you through the controls. White-glove delivery is scheduled by
appointment and is only available within those three metro areas.

TRACKING
You will receive a tracking link by email when your order ships. For help with a
delayed or damaged shipment, contact customer support.
